PROJECT MILESTONECOMPLETED BASELINE

Adult ASHS computational reproduction

A fresh Stage 1–7 Princeton ASHS run reproduced all three online final label maps for one adult case: zero differing voxels and Dice overlap of 1.0 after aligning the label-number convention.

This establishes computational agreement for the tested case. It does not establish anatomical accuracy or generalize the result to a cohort.

LOOKING AHEADPLANNED EXPERIMENT

Motion and MRI image quality

The next planned experiment is to simulate motion using TorchIO and evaluate how ASHS labels change relative to a clean-image baseline. No motion-correction or super-resolution benefit has been established in this project.
